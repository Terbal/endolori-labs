Smart Inbox

The system that turns incoming emails into organized, tracked business requests.

An engineered inbox, not a workaround.

Smart Inbox reads every incoming email, classifies it according to your business rules, files its attachments, updates your Google Sheets, and notifies the right person — automatically.

No new software to learn, no migration, no vendor lock-in. Smart Inbox works with the tools your team already uses every day.
